## 1. EXECUTIVE STRATEGIC BRIEF

### 1.1 Strategic Rationale
**The Adorned Enterprise & Innovation Acceleration Initiative (AIVA)** is an institutional-grade faith-based accelerator and venture seed fund engineered to catalyze high-potential micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) within *The Adorned International* and select external innovators.

Under the direct governance of the **Senior Pastoral Advisory Board**, AIVA establishes a repeatable ecosystem that bridges business literacy, seed capital, executive mentorship, and post-pitch incubation to create sustainable economic value and marketplace impact.

### 1.2 Key Program Parameters & Core Targets
* **Target Inaugural Showcase**: **December 2026 (Q4 Demo Day)**
* **Target Intake**: 50+ Applications; 30 Shortlisted for Bootcamp; Top 5 Selected for Incubation.
* **Participant Split**: **80% Church Members** (verified through fellowship/department leadership) | **20% External Innovators** (community outreach & evangelism engine).
* **Capital Budget**: **₦14,000,000 Total** (₦6.5M Seed Grants | ₦7.5M Operational, Training, Event & Incubation Logistics).
* **Governance**: Direct oversight by the **Senior Pastoral Advisory Board & Fund Investment Committee (FIC)**.

## 2. STRATEGIC FRAMEWORKS & MARKET DIAGNOSTICS

### 2.1 The Small Business Capital & Execution Diagnostic
Analysis of emerging market MSMEs reveals that traditional venture models fail early-stage entrepreneurs due to misaligned incentives and high collateral demands. AIVA solves three critical structural bottlenecks:

```
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         TRADITIONAL VC vs. AIVA MODEL MATRIX                        |
+--------------------------+----------------------------+----------------------------+
| Structural Dimension     | Traditional VC / Bank      | AIVA Initiative            |
+--------------------------+----------------------------+----------------------------+
| Capital Access           | High Collateral / Debt     | Non-Dilutive Grant / Equity|
| Mentorship Model         | Transactional / Passive    | C-Suite Executive Mentors  |
| Ecosystem Alignment      | Pure Commercial Return     | 4-Pillar (Financial/Kingdom|
| Risk Mitigation          | Legal Enforcement          | High-Trust Faith Network   |
| Governance Board         | Investment Fund Partners   | Senior Pastoral Leadership |
+--------------------------+----------------------------+----------------------------+
```

### 2.2 The 3-Horizon Growth Strategy (McKinsey Framework)
To ensure long-term sustainability beyond Cohort 1, AIVA employs McKinsey’s 3-Horizon Growth Model:

* **Horizon 1: Seed & Formalization (Months 1–12 / 2026)**
  * Launch Cohort 1; execute 6-week bootcamp; run December 2026 Demo Day; formalize Top 5 businesses with CAC registration, tax accounts, and financial accounting software.
* **Horizon 2: Scale & Supply Chain Integration (Months 13–24 / 2027)**
  * Integrate funded businesses into the commercial supply chains of established church members' enterprises; initiate the 10% Revolving Seed Fund profit payback model.
* **Horizon 3: The Adorned Kingdom Venture Fund (Months 25–36+ / 2028+)**
  * Expand fund size to ₦50M+ with external impact investor backing; establish an evergreen seed fund that self-funds future cohorts from portfolio capital returns.

## 3. PROGRAM PIPELINE & OPERATIONAL BLUEPRINT

The program follows an 8-month execution runway culminating in the **December 2026 Demo Day**, followed by 6 months of post-pitch incubation.

```
  AUG 2026          SEP 2026          OCT - NOV 2026      DEC 2026         JAN - JUN 2027
+-----------+     +-----------+     +----------------+  +-----------+    +----------------+
|  Board    | --> | Intake &  | --> | 6-Week Bootcamp|->| DEMO DAY  | -->| 6-Month Top 5  |
| Approval  |     | Screening |     |  & Mentorship  |  | SHOWCASE  |    | Incubation Hub |
+-----------+     +-----------+     +----------------+  +-----------+    +----------------+
  Board Setup       Portal Open      Weekend Master-     Live Pitch        C-Suite Mentors
  Sponsor Pitch     80/20 Vetting    class & Pitch Coaching  ₦6.5M Prize   Milestone Disburse
```

### 3.1 Stage 1: Intake & Vetting (September 2026)
* **Intake Target**: 50+ business applications submitted via the AIVA Portal.
* **Selection Rubric (100 Points)**:
  1. *Problem-Solution Fit & Market Size* (30 Pts)
  2. *Founder Competency & Execution History* (25 Pts)
  3. *Financial Viability & Unit Economics* (25 Pts)
  4. *Kingdom Alignment & Ethical Footprint* (20 Pts)

### 3.2 Stage 2: 6-Week Enterprise Bootcamp (October – November 2026)
* **Module 1**: Value Proposition, Customer Discovery & Business Model Canvas
* **Module 2**: Unit Economics, Pricing Models & Financial Forecasting
* **Module 3**: CAC Legal Setup, Tax Compliance & Corporate Governance
* **Module 4**: Digital Marketing, Sales Funnels & Brand Positioning
* **Module 5**: Pitch Deck Mastery & Financial Storytelling

### 3.3 Stage 3: Demo Day Showcase ("The Kingdom Venture Showcase" - December 2026)
* **Format**: Executive Pitch Event held in December 2026 with 5-minute founder presentations followed by 3-minute Q&A before the **Senior Pastoral Leadership & Investment Committee**.
* **Capital Grants**:
  * **1st Place Winner**: **₦5,000,000** Grant + 6-Month Incubation
  * **2nd Place Winner**: **₦1,000,000** Grant + 6-Month Incubation
  * **3rd Place Winner**: **₦500,000** Grant + 6-Month Incubation
  * **4th & 5th Place**: Entry into the 6-Month Incubation Program

### 3.4 Stage 4: 6-Month Top 5 Incubation Program (January – June 2027)
* **C-Suite Mentor Matching**: 1-on-1 bi-weekly execution coaching with senior business executives.
* **Shared Resource Hub**: Legal registration (CAC), accounting software setup, cloud credits.
* **Quarterly Milestone Disbursements**: Seed grant tranches released upon achieving predefined operational KPIs.
