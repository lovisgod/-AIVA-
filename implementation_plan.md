# Implementation Plan: The Adorned International Enterprise & Innovation Initiative (AIVA)

## Executive Summary & Overview
**The Adorned International** is launching a flagship annual entrepreneurship training, funding, and incubation program designed to convert business ideas into sustainable, high-impact enterprises. 

This document outlines the strategic roadmap, operational design, funding architecture, return-on-investment (ROI) models, and deliverable specifications for the initiative. The outputs are designed to McKinsey / Mastercard Advisors presentation and advisory standards, utilizing modern carded design principles, rigorous financial logic, and actionable execution plans.

---

## Key Initiative Specifications

1. **Initiative Name**: **The Adorned Enterprise & Innovation Acceleration Initiative** *(Working Title: "Adorned Incubator & Venture Accelerator" - AIVA / "The Adorned Enterprise Fund")*.
2. **Target Audience**: 
   - Primary: Church Members of The Adorned International (80% allocation).
   - Secondary: Select external entrepreneurs (20% allocation for community outreach & kingdom impact).
3. **Program Structure & Timeline (Annual Cadence)**:
   - **Stage 1: Application & Screening (4 Weeks)** - Idea review & vetting.
   - **Stage 2: Enterprise Bootcamp & Training (6 Weeks)** - Idea validation, business modeling, financial management, pitch readiness.
   - **Stage 3: Demo Day ("The Kingdom Venture Showcase")** - Pitch event with judges, church leadership, and corporate partners.
     - **1st Prize**: ₦5,000,000 (~$3,500) Grant/Seed Funding
     - **2nd Prize**: ₦1,000,000 Seed Funding
     - **3rd Prize**: ₦500,000 Seed Funding
   - **Stage 4: 6-Month Incubation Program (Top 5 Businesses)**: Deeper executive mentorship, workspace/tool credits, legal/tax registration support, investor network matchmaking, and quarterly reporting.
4. **Funding & Capital Sourcing Strategy**:
   - Total Estimated Operating & Seed Capital Budget: **₦12,500,000 - ₦15,000,000 per cycle**.
   - **Sourcing Mix**:
     - Church Empowerment Allocation (20-25%)
     - High-Net-Worth Congregation Angel Network / "Kingdom Angels" (35-40%)
     - Corporate Partnerships & CSR Grants (25-30%)
     - Tokenized Registration / Commitment Fees (5-10%)
5. **4-Pillar ROI Framework**:
   - **Financial ROI**: Tithe & offering expansion from growing enterprises; revolving seed fund option (optional 10% non-dilutive grant payback to replenish future cohorts).
   - **Socio-Economic ROI**: Direct job creation (est. 15-30 jobs in Cohort 1), household income elevation, poverty reduction.
   - **Ecosystem & Brand ROI**: Position The Adorned International as a premier faith-based innovation & economic development hub in West Africa.
   - **Kingdom / Spiritual ROI**: Marketplace evangelism, redemptive business principles, ethical business leadership.

> **Fund Structure (Grant vs. Revolving Seed Fund)**: We recommend a **hybrid seed grant / revolving payback model** where prize money is given as an equity-free grant, but top funded companies agree to give back 5-10% of profits after 18 months into the Next Cohort Fund to ensure multi-year sustainability.
